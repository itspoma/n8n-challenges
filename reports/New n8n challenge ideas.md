# Teach the missing core, verify without mentors

The owner should add ten challenges in two waves, and the first five need no new account beyond n8n except one free model key: **Tidy Up the Festival Program** (items, expressions and Valencia-time dates), **Check In Every Ticket Exactly Once** (a webhook API with authentication, status codes and duplicate-proof storage, which settles backlog idea C04), **Fix the Workflow the Assistant Built** (debugging a pasted, broken workflow), **Who Changed Their Booking?** (Merge and Compare Datasets) and **Read the Supplier Invoices** (PDFs turned into checked data). Wave two adds error outputs with an alerting error workflow, approval before an AI agent acts, sub-workflows, the Code node on Spain's hourly electricity prices, and AI evaluations. The case rests on a lopsided catalogue: **seven of the 13 challenges are AI-first**, while nothing teaches Merge, Switch, the Code node, sub-workflows, error outputs, file handling, webhook authentication or debugging – most of what n8n's free Academy calls its "technical core", and where forum questions, template views and freelance jobs concentrate. Without a mentor, every challenge must be checkable by the learner: printed expected values, curl scripts and evidence inside n8n work on today's static site, and a small browser layer (hashed answers, pasted-workflow checks, live webhook tests) plus the existing challenge 09 provider for signed completion codes would unlock debug-and-fix, case-file and "beat the baseline" formats. A "Start here" ramp and four tracks should replace the build's fixed ten-challenge main list. Before self-paced traffic grows, several existing challenges also need small fixes: 04 and 12 ship solutions that use a router picking a random free model, 11 still says "AI Assistant", and 02 and 13 rely on a Telegram Trigger that fails for self-hosted learners without a public HTTPS address – and, unlike 08, their preparation steps don't say so.

## Seven of 13 challenges teach AI while n8n's technical core goes untaught

The current set teaches a narrow non-AI core well: a GET webhook that returns text, Schedule, Form, Telegram and Tally triggers, the list nodes (Split Out, Filter, Sort, Limit, Aggregate, Remove Duplicates), IF, Data Table inserts, paginated HTTP GETs with Retry On Fail, and one Error Trigger in a bonus. But **04, 06, 08, 10, 11, 12 and 13 are all AI-first**, so a learner who skips AI has six challenges, only three of them beginner ([challenge files](../content/challenges/)). Nothing teaches Merge, Switch, Summarize, Compare Datasets, the Code node, sub-workflows, error outputs or Stop and Error, file conversion, webhook authentication or status codes, human approval, Luxon dates, polling triggers or the debugging toolkit, and expressions such as `$('Node').item` appear in solutions without ever being explained. Mapped against n8n Academy, the site covers N8N101's triggers and mapping and N8N103's AI unit generously, but misses most of N8N102, which n8n calls **"the technical core"** – flow logic, sub-workflows, HTTP methods and authentication, error handling and Data Tables beyond insert ([Academy announcement](https://community.n8n.io/t/299695); [N8N102](https://learn.n8n.io/courses/course-v1:n8n+N8N102+2026H2/about)).

Those gaps sit where learners struggle. AI dominates what gets published – **69% of 13,055 templates** – yet **the Code node appears in 58% of all templates and HTTP Request in 51%** ([template API facets](https://api.n8n.io/api/templates/search?page=1&rows=100)). In the past year's 600 top forum questions, after self-hosting (15.8%), AI agents (14.7%) and credentials (12.2%) come stuck executions (10.3%), binary files and PDFs (7.8%), expressions and JSON (7.7%) and HTTP and APIs (7.5%) ([top questions](https://community.n8n.io/c/questions/12/l/top.json?period=yearly)); all-time tags put data-transformation (1,981), http-request (1,443), credentials (972) and webhook (667) near the top ([tags.json](https://community.n8n.io/tags.json)). Video serves these skills poorly – error-handling tutorials total about 214k views against 4.3M for self-hosting ([YouTube: error handling](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=n8n+error+handling); [YouTube: self-hosting](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=n8n+self+host)) – and the official video course falls from 47,660 to 5,812 forum views by part five, a sign that self-paced learners finish standalone units rather than series ([forum top topics](https://community.n8n.io/top.json?period=all)). The forum is also shifting: **44% of the 750 newest questions carry the `bug:unconfirmed` tag** ([latest questions](https://community.n8n.io/c/questions/12/l/latest.json?order=created)), and last month's new questions ran at about a third of the past year's monthly average – which may reflect a category change, since the research could not tell ([categories](https://community.n8n.io/categories.json)) – while the n8n Assistant now plans, builds and fixes workflows inside the product ([changelog](https://docs.n8n.io/changelog/readme.md)). Learners increasingly need to read, debug and verify workflows, not just assemble them.

The competitive frame changed in 2026. n8n Academy opened free courses with hosted practice APIs on 16 June ([Academy announcement](https://community.n8n.io/t/299695)); the Community Weekly Challenge, launched on 7 September, gives per-user datasets, auto-checked answers, time ranking and a no-Code-node rule ([announcement](https://community.n8n.io/t/introducing-the-n8n-community-weekly-challenge-a-new-automation-problem-every-monday/311603); [Challenge 01](https://community.n8n.io/t/community-weekly-challenge-01-is-live-the-night-gate/313635)); NodeQuest offers 28 free "fix the broken workflow" levels ([forum](https://community.n8n.io/t/nodequest-learn-n8n-by-fixing-broken-workflows-28-puzzle-levels/302950)); and Spain's n8n Camp charges €390–€540 for 15 corrected challenges ([n8n.camp](https://n8n.camp/)). None offers free, untimed, trilingual business-story challenges that mix real services and AI. The site's niche is therefore evergreen, Valencia-flavoured practice a learner can check alone, framed as "practice after N8N101 or N8N102" rather than a rival course, with a link to the Weekly Challenge for anyone who wants competition.

The ten recommendations apply the backlog's own selection criteria, updated for online use: concept gap first, then demand, then friction (no new account, or only a free model key; no Google OAuth, WhatsApp or self-hosted Telegram webhooks), reliable data (site fixtures or official APIs queried for past dates), an outcome the learner can check without a mentor, translation cost and overlap ([backlog](../docs/CHALLENGE_IDEA_BACKLOG.md)). Ranked on demand alone, the list would open with self-hosting and WhatsApp: self-hosting is the largest forum bucket, and "Building Your First WhatsApp Chatbot" is the third most-viewed template at **331,713 views** ([template](https://n8n.io/workflows/2465)). Both drop back only because a self-paced learner cannot yet complete or verify them reliably, not for lack of interest. The AI share stays deliberate: three of the ten are AI challenges, aimed at the patterns the existing seven skip – documents, human approval and measurement.

### 1. Tidy Up the Festival Program teaches the item model behind every expression

Expressions hold every existing solution together – `$('…').item` appears in 03, 04, 11 and 13 and `.first()` in 02, 08 and 09 – yet no challenge explains the item model behind them. That makes it the gap with the widest reach: **data-transformation is the forum's fifth-largest tag at 1,981 topics, with expressions adding 336** ([tags.json](https://community.n8n.io/tags.json)), the interactive "Learn JSON Basics" template has **74,178 views** ([template API](https://api.n8n.io/api/templates/search?page=1&rows=100&sort=views:desc)), and help threads ask basics such as how Split Out reaches into an array ([forum](https://community.n8n.io/t/split-out-node-how-to-get-data-from-array/219321)). Dates belong in the same challenge because n8n falls back to **New York time when neither the workflow nor the instance sets a time zone** ([Date & Time](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.datetime)), a silent bug in every Valencia scenario. The bonus teaches `.first()` and `.all()`, which n8n 3.0 makes the standard fix by removing `$getPairedItem` ([v3.0 breaking changes](https://docs.n8n.io/changelog/v30-breaking-changes)), and links to the owner's own item-linking article ([blog](../content/blog/en/article-630cee15-f31a-4e34-be1c-fb4015601bd9.md)). The program is a synthetic file on the site, so nothing can rate-limit or change, and the page can print exact expected rows.

| Field | Specification |
|---|---|
| Level · complexity · time | Beginner · 2/5 ★★ · 20–30 min |
| Concept | How data moves between nodes as items, and how expressions read nested fields, earlier nodes and dates |
| Scenario | A festival office turning its program feed into newsletter rows; a venue converting UTC start times into Valencia time for posters; a social-media team listing free events with performer counts |
| Task | "Our festival program arrives as one tangled file. Comms needs one tidy line per event – title, venue and district, local start time, length in minutes, number of performers and whether it's free – ready for the newsletter." |
| Bonus task | Add the program's version and total event count to every row, fix the "multiple matching items" error this causes by reading the header with `.first()` or `.all()`, and explain the cause in one sentence |
| Nodes | Manual Trigger, HTTP Request, Split Out, Edit Fields (Set), Date & Time, Filter, Aggregate |
| Before you start | No account beyond n8n (Cloud trial, or self-hosted with Docker). The program is a site fixture such as `/fixtures/festival-program.json` (to be created). Set the workflow time zone to Europe/Madrid |
| Requirements | (1) The workflow returns exactly one row per event – the page states how many – and every row has all seven fields.<br>(2) Start times read as Valencia time in dd/MM HH:mm, including the two events either side of the October clock change.<br>(3) The free-events view holds exactly the stated number of rows, and two named events match the expected rows on the page character for character |
| Verify without a mentor | Today: compare with the expected rows and counts printed on the page; the clock-change events expose any time-zone slip. With the checker: paste the last node's output for a field-by-field diff, plus a checksum from a second fixture the page never shows, so hand-edited output fails |
| Format · track | Match the target output · Data handling |

### 2. Check In Every Ticket Exactly Once turns backlog C04 into a real API

Challenge 01 teaches a GET webhook that returns text; real webhooks receive POSTs that must authenticate the caller, answer with meaningful status codes and survive retries. **"Creating an API endpoint" is the second most-viewed of 13,055 n8n templates, at 422,411 views** ([template](https://n8n.io/workflows/1750)); webhooks make up 4.7–7.2% of forum questions ([top questions](https://community.n8n.io/c/questions/12/l/top.json?period=yearly); [latest questions](https://community.n8n.io/c/questions/12/l/latest.json?order=created)); and n8n Academy builds an exercise around a webhook-driven order system ([forum search](https://community.n8n.io/search?q=exercises)). Every piece is native: the Webhook node offers Header, Basic and JWT authentication ([Webhook](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.webhook)), Respond to Webhook returns JSON with any status code and headers ([Respond to Webhook](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.respondtowebhook)), and since 2.3 the Data Table node can branch on "If Row Does Not Exist" or upsert ([Data Table](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.datatable)). The challenge answers the open questions in backlog idea C04 – storage, authentication and scope ([backlog](../docs/CHALLENGE_IDEA_BACKLOG.md)) – and folds in Switch, another missing node. Because the learner's own curl calls the workflow, **self-hosted learners need no tunnel**.

| Field | Specification |
|---|---|
| Level · complexity · time | Intermediate · 3/5 ★★★ · 30–45 min |
| Concept | A webhook as a small, safe API: methods, header authentication, status codes and duplicate-proof storage |
| Scenario | Door scanners at a Valencia concert that resend scans over patchy Wi-Fi; a gym checking membership cards at the door; a workshop's badge check-in |
| Task | "Our door scanners send you each ticket code – sometimes twice when the Wi-Fi drops. Accept only our scanners, reject empty scans, let each ticket in once, and tell staff clearly when a ticket has already been used." |
| Bonus task | Also reject scans whose `X-Signature` header isn't the HMAC-SHA256 of the request body, using the Crypto node with a Crypto credential |
| Nodes | Webhook, Switch, Data Table, Edit Fields (Set), Respond to Webhook, Crypto |
| Before you start | No external account. A terminal with curl. Create a Data Table `checkins` and a Header Auth credential with a token you choose. On n8n Cloud, publish the workflow to get its production URL; on self-hosted n8n, curl reaches `localhost` directly |
| Requirements | (1) A scan without the scanners' token, or with an empty ticket code, is refused with an error status and stores nothing.<br>(2) A ticket's first scan answers 201 with `{"status":"checked_in"}`; every repeat answers 200 with `already_checked_in` and the time of the first scan.<br>(3) After the five test scans on the page, the check-in table holds exactly two rows |
| Verify without a mentor | Today: five copy-paste curl commands, each with its expected status and body. With the checker: the organizer workflow sends the same scans to the learner's production URL from the server side and returns a signed code; a test run from the browser must wait until CORS preflight for custom headers is confirmed |
| Format · track | Standard build with scripted tests · Integrations & APIs |

### 3. Fix the Workflow the Assistant Built makes debugging the lesson

Debugging is the skill every learner without a mentor needs, and no challenge teaches it. **44% of the 750 newest forum questions are tagged as unconfirmed bugs, and 11.5% concern stuck, slow or non-triggering executions** ([latest questions](https://community.n8n.io/c/questions/12/l/latest.json?order=created)). More workflows are now generated: the n8n Assistant, on Cloud since 9 July 2026, supersedes the AI Workflow Builder ([changelog](https://docs.n8n.io/changelog/readme.md)), and "Stop Learning n8n in 2026" drew **488,003 views** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZeJXI2MAhj0)), so reviewing generated work is the durable skill. NodeQuest's 28 levels already use the format, though no player numbers are public ([forum](https://community.n8n.io/t/nodequest-learn-n8n-by-fixing-broken-workflows-28-puzzle-levels/302950)), and studies find that learning from erroneous examples improves when self-explanation prompts or explanations accompany them ([Frontiers in Psychology 2022](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/psychology/articles/10.3389/fpsyg.2022.1032003/full)), so the page should reveal a short "why it broke" after each fix. The starter plants three bugs drawn from forum pain – an item-linking error after Aggregate, a missing workflow time zone and a Filter comparing the text "10" with a number – two of which revisit #1, doubling as retrieval practice. It runs offline on pinned data, which n8n uses only in manual development runs ([Pin and mock data](https://docs.n8n.io/build/work-with-data/pin-and-mock-data.md)).

### 4. Who Changed Their Booking? covers Merge and Compare Datasets

Combining sources is the next most useful data skill, and no challenge touches it. **"Joining different datasets" has 122,191 views** ([template](https://n8n.io/workflows/1747)), merge has 202 forum topics on top of data-transformation's 1,981 ([tags.json](https://community.n8n.io/tags.json)), and threads such as "Loop Over Items can't handle multiple incoming lists?" (606 views, 27 posts) show learners reaching for loops where Merge belongs ([forum](https://community.n8n.io/t/loop-over-items-cant-handle-multiple-incoming-lists/234133)). Merge's modes – Append, Combine by Matching Fields with Enrich or Keep Non-Matches, Combine by Position, SQL Query, Choose Branch – are easy to confuse ([Merge](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.merge)), which is exactly where practising similar-looking concepts side by side produces the largest gains ([Brunmair & Richter 2019](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/335004545_Similarity_matters_A_meta-analysis_of_interleaved_learning_and_its_moderators)). Compare Datasets adds four outputs – in A only, same, different, in B only ([Compare Datasets](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.comparedatasets)). Three small fixture files make the answer deterministic and almost language-neutral, and the owner's Merge article becomes the reading link ([blog](../content/blog/en/article-ac9d26c4-8b42-4332-9b67-ffed8e320b2d.md)). The Weekly Challenge works in the same data-cleanup territory, so this challenge stays untimed and story-led rather than copying its ranking.

| Task | "Every morning we get yesterday's and today's attendee lists plus our ticket-type list. Tell the front desk, in one message, who is new, who cancelled, who changed their booking and how many stayed the same – with each person's ticket type." |
| Bonus task | Redo the ticket-type enrichment with Merge's SQL Query mode, and make `ANA@EXAMPLE.COM` match `ana@example.com` |

| Before you start | No account. Three fixture files on the site: yesterday's attendees, today's attendees and ticket types. The message can stay inside n8n; sending it is optional |
| Requirements | (1) Every attendee on today's list carries the right ticket type, with nobody dropped or duplicated.<br>(2) Attendees are sorted into new, cancelled, changed and unchanged, and the four counts match the page.<br>(3) One ready-to-send message gives the four counts and names everyone who changed |
| Verify without a mentor | Today: the page prints the four counts and the IDs that changed. With the checker: the learner types the changed IDs, the page compares a salted hash so it never prints the answer, and seeded variants make copied answers fail |

### 5. Read the Supplier Invoices fills the largest AI demand the site ignores

Documents are the biggest AI use case the site never touches. **Document Ops alone holds 1,822 templates**, with 1,216 more under Document Extraction and 369 under Invoice Processing ([template categories](https://api.n8n.io/api/templates/categories)); "Extract text from a PDF file" has **96,182 views** ([template API](https://api.n8n.io/api/templates/search?page=1&rows=100&sort=views:desc)); binary files and PDFs make up **7.8% of the past year's top forum questions** ([top questions](https://community.n8n.io/c/questions/12/l/top.json?period=yearly)); and an RFQ-to-quote job post drew 4,920 views and 363 posts ([forum](https://community.n8n.io/t/seeking-estimates-n8n-developer-for-a-small-rfq-to-quote-prototype/312281)). Supply is thin, with invoice and OCR tutorials totalling about 170k YouTube views ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=n8n+invoice)). The challenge also covers the missing file-handling skill through Extract from File's PDF operation ([Extract from File](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.extractfromfile.md)) and introduces the Information Extractor ([Information Extractor](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/cluster-nodes/root-nodes/n8n-nodes-langchain.information-extractor.md)), a chain that sidesteps the documented unreliability of structured output on agents ([common issues](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/cluster-nodes/sub-nodes/n8n-nodes-langchain.outputparserstructured/common-issues.md)). Synthetic Spanish invoices on the site keep the answers exact; tips should ask the model for plain decimals, because formats like "1.234,56 €" trip free models. A full run is estimated at **about $0.012 on gpt-4o-mini** and nothing on Gemini's free tier ([OpenRouter models](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models)).

### 6. Don't Let One Bad Record Stop the Batch teaches failure by design

Challenge 09 teaches retries and leaves the Error Trigger to a bonus; nothing teaches per-item error outputs, deliberate failures with Stop and Error, or alerting – the try/catch patterns of N8N102 and N8N103 ([N8N103](https://learn.n8n.io/courses/course-v1:n8n+N8N103+2026H2/about)). Demand is steady rather than loud: a thread on monitoring production workflows drew 1,447 views ([forum](https://community.n8n.io/t/how-do-you-monitor-n8n-workflows-in-production-and-detect-failures-early/300723)), error-handling tutorials total about 214k YouTube views ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=n8n+error+handling)), and the owner's own article names error handling "the non-negotiable baseline" ([blog](../content/blog/en/article-54bc6674-c42b-4d0f-a2b9-d1b4ba98591e.md)); freelance job threads that draw hundreds of applicants reward this production-grade skill over demos. Two facts shape the design. Error workflows **run only on automatic executions, never on manual tests** ([Error Trigger](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.errortrigger)), so the learner publishes a webhook and calls it; and the site's GitHub Pages host returns a **real HTTP 404 for a missing fixture** ([live check](https://n8n-challenges.app/fixtures/does-not-exist-INV-1013.json)), so "missing file" failures need no backend. The alert goes to a no-signup ntfy.sh topic, which allows 250 messages a day per IP address ([ntfy limits](https://ntfy.sh/v1/account)).

| Nodes | Webhook, HTTP Request, IF, Data Table, Summarize, Stop and Error, Error Trigger |

### 10. Test Your AI Before You Trust It measures instead of eyeballing

Every AI challenge on the site ends when an output looks right; none asks whether it is reliably right. n8n has shipped evaluations since May 2025 ([release notes 1.x](https://docs.n8n.io/changelog/release-notes-1.x.md)), including a Categorization metric that scores exact matches ([use metrics](https://docs.n8n.io/build/integrate-ai/test-and-improve-ai-workflows/use-metrics-to-measure-quality.md)). Template supply is tiny – **six evaluation templates among the 1,200 most-viewed AI templates** ([templates API](https://api.n8n.io/api/templates/search?page=1&rows=50&category=AI&sort=views:desc)) – but the feature's beta thread drew 138 posts ([forum](https://community.n8n.io/t/help-us-test-the-new-ai-evaluation-feature/118962)) and complaints such as agents finishing "green" after a failed tool are common ([forum](https://community.n8n.io/t/ai-agent-node-finishes-green-even-when-its-tool-failed-how-i-catch-it-now/300885)), so the site can lead here rather than follow. ## Learners can check their own work on a five-rung ladder

Self-paced platforms verify work in four ways: a known answer checked mechanically, as with Advent of Code's per-user puzzle inputs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advent_of_Code)); tests run against the learner's artifact, as when freeCodeCamp's browser tests call the learner's own project URL ([GitHub issue #10901](https://github.com/freeCodeCamp/freeCodeCamp/issues/10901)); end-state checks, as in Microsoft's Applied Skills labs, which score results rather than methods ([Microsoft Learn](https://learn.microsoft.com/credentials/support/assessment-duration-lab-experience)); and human judgment, which the site no longer has. Every card above therefore names an outcome the learner can observe today – values printed on the page, curl responses, Data Table rows, execution statuses – which matters because novices overestimate their own performance ([Kruger & Dunning 1999](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.77.6.1121)) and rubrics help mainly by making criteria explicit ([Panadero & Jonsson 2013](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.edurev.2013.01.002)). When printing an answer would spoil a puzzle, learners can hash a compound answer inside n8n with the Crypto node's SHA-256 Hash action and compare it with a hash on the page ([Crypto](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.crypto/)), which needs no site code at all. Above that floor, the research proposes a ladder of browser and organizer checks.

| Rung | Method | What the site needs | Best for | Resistance to copying |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Self-check rubric: each requirement becomes a checkbox with an observable test; "solved" is saved in localStorage | Replace the "Find a mentor" dialog | Everything, and the only option for Telegram, vision and MCP outcomes | Honour system |
| 1 | Answer check: a normalised, salted SHA-256 compared in the browser, with seeded variants per learner | Answer box and a build-time variant generator | New #4, #5, #9, #10; existing 07, 10, 11 | Medium; compound answers resist guessing |
| 2 | Workflow check: the learner pastes workflow JSON and the page checks node types, counts and wiring locally | A client-side parser | New #3, #8; Parsons and remix formats | Medium |
| 3 | Live test: the page calls the learner's published webhook and checks the responses | CORS on the learner's Webhook (default `*`) | Existing 01; GET-based variants of new #2 | High for behaviour |
| 4 | Organizer checker: one n8n workflow, reusing 09's provider, runs hidden tests and returns a signed completion code the browser verifies | Uptime, rate limits, privacy | New #2, #6; existing 09; hidden-test AI challenges | High |

Rungs 1–3 run entirely in the browser with the Web Crypto API ([MDN](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/SubtleCrypto/digest)) and localStorage, so GitHub Pages hosting is unaffected, but the site plan still limits client-side JavaScript to theme, tips and the review dialog and assigns verification to mentors ([site plan](../docs/N8N_CHALLENGES_PLAN.md)); that rule needs amending first. Three behaviours need a prototype. The Webhook node's Allowed Origins setting defaults to `*` for non-preflight requests ([Webhook](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.webhook/)), but whether it answers the OPTIONS preflight that custom headers and JSON bodies trigger is unverified, so live tests should start with simple GETs. Since Chrome 142, a public page calling `localhost` triggers a permission prompt ([gHacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2025/10/29/google-chrome-142-restricts-local-network-access-and-changes-sync-on-desktop/)), so self-hosted learners keep curl as their path. And signed codes depend on n8n's Crypto or JWT node producing a signature Web Crypto can verify ([JWT node](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/core-nodes/n8n-nodes-base.jwt.md)), which nobody has tested. freeCodeCamp's experience – forum reports of verification passing "with any URL" ([forum](https://forum.freecodecamp.org/t/project-solution-verification-works-with-any-url/423853)) – argues for asserting on response bodies that contain per-learner values. Seeded variants should aim to stop copied answers, not attackers, because on a self-paced site cheating mostly cheats the learner – the point Advent of Code's FAQ makes by comparing AI-solved puzzles to sending a friend to the gym for you ([AoC](https://adventofcode.com/2025/about)). Signed codes and answers can be exported as a small "progress passport" file to move between devices without accounts.

Nine formats from successful hands-on platforms transfer to a static n8n site, and the plan uses each where it fits.

| Format | Precedent | Where it appears in this plan |
|---|---|---|
| Debug-and-fix: paste a workflow with planted bugs | NodeQuest; find-the-error research | #3; The Greeting Is Broken; later Fix the Broken Agent |
| Match the target output | Frontend Mentor's design-to-code projects; Kaggle Learn's `check()` | #1, #9 |
| Case file with one checkable answer | SQL Murder Mystery; Advent of Code | #4; later a delivery mystery with a part two |

| Beat the baseline | Kaggle's hidden test split; n8n evaluations | #10 |

| Predict, run, modify | PRIMM and use-modify-create | Read Before You Build |

The evidence favours the cheap formats for beginners: two-dimensional Parsons problems with distractors took significantly less time than fixing or writing code, with no significant difference in learning or one-week retention ([Ericson, Margulieux & Rick 2017](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/320850846_Solving_parsons_problems_versus_fixing_and_writing_code)), and PRIMM builds on findings that learners who can read code write it better ([PRIMM portal](https://primmportal.com/research/)). The hidden test set behind "beat the baseline" exists because people overfit to visible feedback ([Blum & Hardt 2015](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v37/blum15.pdf)). Four formats need one change to the content format: **a "Starter workflow" block of copy-paste JSON inside the challenge Markdown**, because the README forbids standalone workflow files ([README](../content/challenges/README.md)) and n8n 3.0 removes editor import from URL while keeping copy-paste and Import from File ([v3.0 breaking changes](https://docs.n8n.io/changelog/v30-breaking-changes)). Whether pasted JSON carries pinned data was not verified; if it doesn't, a starter should read its data from a site fixture. Exported workflows contain credential names and IDs but no secrets, although nodes imported from cURL can carry authentication headers ([export and import](https://docs.n8n.io/build/manage-workflows/export-and-import.md)), so the workflow checker should warn before anything is shared. Leaderboards, speedruns and weekly live contests are the formats to skip: Advent of Code dropped its global leaderboard in 2025 after ten years, calling it one of the largest sources of stress and citing abuse ([AoC](https://adventofcode.com/2025/about)), and n8n's Weekly Challenge already ranks by time.

Data choice decides whether a check survives. Official APIs queried for past dates – REE prices, the National Bank of Ukraine's dated exchange rates, BOE summaries by date – return answers that never change, so the page can print them with no fixture to maintain ([NBU historical rate](https://bank.gov.ua/NBUStatService/v1/statdirectory/exchange?valcode=USD&date=20261001&json)). Live-only sources need snapshot fixtures on the site, which GitHub Pages serves with `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` and a 10-minute cache ([live probe](https://n8n-challenges.app/)) under a 100 GB monthly soft bandwidth limit ([GitHub Pages limits](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits)). Error drills need either an importable mock-API workflow, which keeps working when the organizer's server is down, or public simulators such as DummyJSON's `/http/{code}` and `?delay=` endpoints ([DummyJSON docs](https://dummyjson.com/docs)); hosted mocks with tiny quotas, such as Beeceptor's 50 requests per endpoint per day, would collapse at an event ([Beeceptor pricing](https://beeceptor.com/pricing/)). Fixtures in the public repo should be synthetic, or small openly licensed snapshots with attribution – never copies of Idealista, Mercadona or EMT pages. For Valencia, the old OpenDataSoft portal now returns 404 ([probe](https://valencia.opendatasoft.com/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets?limit=1)), and the live backbone is the city's CKAN catalogue with keyless, CC-BY geoportal layers such as Valenbisi's 273 stations, updated within the minute ([Valenbisi layer](https://geoportal.valencia.es/server/rest/services/OPENDATA/Trafico/MapServer/228?f=json)). One trap for future date challenges: Nager.Date omits Valencia's 19 March holiday, which the Generalitat's 2026 calendar includes ([Nager.Date](https://date.nager.at/api/v3/PublicHolidays/2026/ES); [GVA](https://comunica.gva.es/es/detalle?id=394003553&site=373430869)).

AI challenges need one default model path a learner can follow alone. The Gemini API free tier needs no card, but users must be 18 or older, and in the EEA the paid-tier data terms apply even to unpaid quota; a public-facing bot for EEA users would need paid services ([Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms)). OpenRouter's free models allow 20 requests a minute and 50 a day, rising to 1,000 after a lifetime $10 purchase ([OpenRouter limits](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api-reference/limits)) – tight when an agent makes roughly three to eight model calls per turn, a research estimate. Groq's free plan allows 30 requests a minute and 1,000 a day on its gpt-oss models but only 8,000 tokens a minute ([Groq limits](https://console.groq.com/docs/rate-limits)), and n8n Cloud trials include Gateway credits that need no provider account but cannot be topped up during the trial ([Gateway credits](https://docs.n8n.io/deploy/use-n8n-cloud/gateway-credits.md)). For learners who pay, gpt-4o-mini at $0.15 in and $0.60 out per million tokens keeps most challenges to a few cents ([OpenRouter models](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models)). Each AI challenge should name one tool-capable model, keep model calls out of loops, and be smoke-tested with that model before publishing.

Tips replace the mentor, so their design matters. In tutoring-system logs, students viewed **68% of the earlier hint levels for less than a second** on their way to the answer-giving hint ([Aleven et al. 2016](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40593-015-0089-1)). The site's five tips should carry visible type labels – Direction, Nodes, Flow, Configuration and Near-solution, as defined in the archived event plan ([deferred plan](../docs/DEFERRED_PLATFORM_PLAN.md)) – so learners pick the help they need, with a short pause ("write down what you tried") before the near-solution tip and a private "solved with 0 of 5 tips" mark instead of a penalty. Revealing the solution images before solving can mark a challenge "solved with solution", as Codewars does when a learner unlocks solutions ([Codewars docs](https://docs.codewars.com/concepts/kata/solutions/)). Rewards should stay light: gamification's effects are small, and only the effect on learning outcomes held up in rigorous studies ([Sailer & Homner 2020](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1245270)); badges did raise activity in a two-year field experiment ([Hamari 2017](https://research.aalto.fi/en/publications/do-badges-increase-user-activity-a-field-experiment-on-the-effect/)), but broken streaks demotivate out of proportion to what was missed ([Silverman & Barasch 2023](https://www.insead.edu/faculty-research/publications/journal-articles/or-track-how-broken-streaks-affect-consumer)). Track badges in localStorage fit; daily streaks and leaderboards don't. All of this evidence comes from mathematics, programming and general education research – no study covers learning low-code automation directly, so its transfer to n8n is an inference.

## A Start-here ramp and four tracks should replace the fixed ten

The build currently requires exactly ten challenges in the main collection and shows the rest under "More challenges" ([README](../content/challenges/README.md)). That suited a hackathon with ten balloons; with 20-plus challenges and visitors arriving from search, it hides the path. The research proposes a short Start-here ramp, four topic tracks and cross-track capstones, joined by soft prerequisites – one to three concept tags per challenge and at most two "recommended before" links, as in Exercism's concept tree ([Exercism Python track](https://exercism.org/tracks/python)) – never hard locks, plus a one-line "New to n8n? Start here (about 40 minutes)" banner on pages reached from search. In the content model that means new `track`, `tags` and `recommendedBefore` metadata and dropping the exactly-ten rule, while keeping slugs stable for existing links. Unlocking belongs only inside campaigns, where part two opens after part one, as CTFd's unlockable challenges work ([CTFd](https://docs.ctfd.io/tutorials/challenges/creating-an-unlockable-challenge/)).

Frontend Mentor's recommended path – 5–10 newbie, 5–8 junior, 3–5 intermediate, then 2–3 advanced projects – puts more practice at the bottom ([Frontend Mentor](https://www.frontendmentor.io/guides/difficulty-levels)), and the research's target of roughly 40/35/25 at about 30 challenges means the third wave should add advanced work: the multi-agent planner, the protected-API challenge and the hardening remix. Levels can borrow n8n Academy's meaning – beginner for QS101 and N8N101 skills, intermediate for N8N102, advanced for N8N103 ([n8n Education](https://n8n.io/education/)) – so each page can say "practice after N8N102" and turn the Academy into a feeder rather than a competitor. Inside every track the same sequence fades support: a worked example, a completion problem, an independent build, a remix or match-the-output, a debug or hardening task, then an open build or campaign episode, following evidence that worked examples help early and should give way to problem solving ([Renkl & Atkinson 2003](https://www.semanticscholar.org/paper/Structuring-the-Transition-From-Example-Study-to-in-Renkl-Atkinson/03a9b918adff333db5d7d960f9b9a6b8128fe34f)). Because guidance that helps novices can hinder experienced learners ([Kalyuga et al. 2003](https://www.scirp.org/reference/referencespapers?referenceid=747628)), a "challenge mode" toggle should hide the node list and tips. Intermediate and advanced pages can invite a ten-minute attempt before tips, since solving before instruction beat the reverse (g = 0.36) except for young learners and general skills ([Sinha & Kapur 2021](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1308129)). A mixed review at the end of each level – only a business task and three requirements, combining concepts from different tracks – adds retrieval practice ([Roediger & Karpicke 2006](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x)).

The ramp costs little because three of its four items reuse existing content; the first three take a newcomer from the canvas to a first debug in about 40 minutes, and the fourth opens the AI track.

Several high-demand ideas should wait, for friction rather than lack of interest. WhatsApp (backlog C09 and C18) draws the strongest interest among Spanish-speaking learners – a Spanish WhatsApp chatbot tutorial has **547,985 views** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M5ZSjBnACtM)) – but n8n's credentials need a Meta developer account and a business portfolio ([n8n WhatsApp credentials](https://docs.n8n.io/integrations/builtin/credentials/whatsapp/)), and secondary sources report test numbers limited to five recipients and temporary tokens lasting under a day ([Digital Inspiration](https://digitalinspiration.com/docs/document-studio/apps/whatsapp/phone)). "Ask the Numbers" delivers C18's value over chat now, and a WhatsApp agent channel merged into n8n on 6 October 2026, release status unknown, is the trigger to revisit ([PR #38750](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n/pull/38750)). Self-hosting is the largest forum bucket and the top YouTube topic, but it is OS-specific, may need a paid server and resists verification, so it suits an article – and 3.0 will require Docker for self-hosting anyway ([v3.0](https://docs.n8n.io/changelog/v30-breaking-changes)). The calendar assistant (C19) waits on Google OAuth, whose Testing-mode refresh tokens expire after seven days ([Google OAuth](https://developers.google.com/identity/protocols/oauth2)). Standalone n8n Agents, in preview since 21 September 2026, are still changing release by release and would churn three translations ([changelog](https://docs.n8n.io/changelog/readme.md); [release notes](https://docs.n8n.io/changelog/release-notes.md)). Voice receptionists, AI video and image generation, Postgres, the n8n API (unavailable on the Cloud trial; [n8n API](https://docs.n8n.io/connect/n8n-api)) and the Ahrefs pipeline (C06) fail on friction, cost or verification. Live air-raid alert data should stay out entirely: the main API requires a manually issued token ([probe](https://api.alerts.in.ua/v1/alerts/active.json)), and turning alerts into a puzzle would trivialise danger for Ukrainian learners, while exchange rates and holidays give Ukrainian flavour without that risk.

## Conclusion

The research shifts the question from "which topics are popular" to "what can a learner finish and prove alone". In 2026 n8n itself began giving away structured courses, weekly auto-checked puzzles and an assistant that writes workflows, so the scarce thing a free community site can offer is no longer another AI demo but practice in the judgment those tools don't supply: reading data, combining sources, making workflows fail safely, keeping a person in charge of an agent and measuring whether AI output is right. That is why debugging and evaluation – formats that treat generated work as something to inspect – rank higher here than their template counts alone would suggest, and why a trilingual dataset can become a test rather than a translation cost.

The highest-leverage work is not any single challenge but two small platform changes, a copy-paste starter-workflow block and a browser check layer, because each unlocks several formats and upgrades existing challenges too; both should be built against n8n 3.0 so nothing is written twice. The main uncertainties are practical. None of the ten challenges has been built, so times, levels and costs are estimates; several behaviours – CORS preflight on webhooks, pinned data in pasted JSON, the human-review steps on 2.42, signature compatibility for completion codes – need a prototype before tips are written; and without privacy-friendly completion data, the owner cannot yet see where learners actually drop off, which is the evidence the third wave should be chosen on.
