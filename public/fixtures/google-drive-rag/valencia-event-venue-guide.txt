Valencia Automation Day – Venue Guide

The registration desk opens at 08:30 in Orange Hall.
The opening session begins at 09:15 in the Main Auditorium.
Workshops begin at 09:30 in Rooms Green 1, Green 2, and Green 3.
Lunch begins at 13:30 in the Courtyard.
The quiet room is Blue Room 2 and remains available from 09:00 until 17:00.
The venue Wi-Fi network is ValenciaAutomationGuest. The password is printed on each attendee badge.
The first-aid point is beside the Orange Hall registration desk.
